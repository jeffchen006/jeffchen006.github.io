Ph.D. Candidate · University of Toronto

Zhiyang Chen

I am visiting the Advanced Software Technologies Lab at ETH Zurich, hosted by Prof. Zhendong Su. I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Toronto, advised by Prof. Fan Long in the PROSE Lab.

My research focuses on LLM security and blockchain security. I have worked on problems in LLM-assisted code generation, DeFi protocols, rollups, and prediction markets. More broadly, I have also collaborated on compilers, program analysis, and CUDA kernel generation.

My Past Experience

Industry Experience

I spent one year as a part-time Research Engineer at Zircuit, working with Dr. Z, Dr. Martin Derka, and Dr. Jan Gorzny. This was invaluable start-up experience: I joined an early-stage team building a complex zk-rollup from scratch, worked through fast research and engineering cycles, and learned from exceptional colleagues and the broader global Web3 community.

Before that, I spent more than one year at Quantstamp, an established blockchain-security company, where I worked with Dr. Martin Derka on smart-contract auditing and automated vulnerability detection. This gave me experience in a stable, mature security organization with rigorous engineering practices, and it was where I helped bring my FlashSyn research into industrial practice.

Our new preprint establishes S(6) ≥ 537, improving the lower bound of 536 from 2000 by explicitly partitioning the integers 1 through 537 into six sum-free sets. We also report 131 pairwise inequivalent partitions, with certificates and verification code publicly available.
